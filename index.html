Adam

A private, personal voice assistant for one person's iPhone.

Adam is a personal iOS app, not offered to the public. It listens and answers by voice and runs its language model entirely on the iPhone.

With the owner's permission, Adam can read and summarize the owner's Gmail, and, only after the owner approves each action on screen, send a reply or new email, archive, label, mark as read or unread, or move a conversation to Trash.
