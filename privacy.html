Privacy policy

Adam, a personal iOS app. Last updated October 5, 2026.

Who uses Adam

Adam is a personal app used by its owner on their own iPhone. It is not distributed to other people.

What Adam accesses

  • Gmail, only after the owner connects it and approves Google's permission screen: Adam reads, searches and summarizes email, and, only after the owner approves each specific action, sends email, archives, labels, marks as read or unread, or moves conversations to Trash. Adam cannot permanently delete email or change the Google account.
  • Microphone and speech recognition, to understand spoken requests. Recognition runs on the iPhone.
  • Calendar, reminders and photos, only when the owner asks and grants iOS permission.

Where data goes

Adam has no server. Email content is fetched directly from Google to the iPhone and processed there by an on-device language model. Adam does not send Gmail data, voice, or any other personal data to its developer or to any third party, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Google sign-in tokens are stored only in the iPhone's Keychain. Nothing is kept on any server.

Google user data

Adam's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect Gmail at any time in Adam (Settings, Skills, Gmail, Disconnect), which revokes access and deletes the stored tokens, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app deletes all its data on the iPhone.
